The injury was visible in the data three weeks earlier.

We build the analytics layer for clubs: wearable and video data turned into workload management, injury risk and squad decisions. Running as a pilot with a Turkish Basketball Super League club and its football side.

The problem

The data exists. It just never reaches the person making the decision.

Heart-rate belts, GPS vests and match footage all produce numbers, each in its own application, each exported to a spreadsheet nobody opens on match day. The coach decides on feel, and the physiotherapist finds out afterwards.

What we build

Injury risk

Workload that warns before it breaks

Acute and chronic load tracked per athlete, with the ratio between them watched over time. The output is not a chart nobody reads; it is a list of names that need attention this week.

  • Heart rate and heart-rate variability from team wearables
  • Accelerometer and positional load from GPS vests
  • Load calendar across the season, per athlete and per squad
Video

Match footage read automatically

Full-match video processed into events, positions and phases. What took an analyst a night takes the system the length of the match.

Squad

Depth and scouting on one screen

Where the squad is thin, who covers which role, and which targets fit the gap, built on league data rather than opinion.

Commercial

Sponsor visibility, measured

How long each sponsor was actually on screen, in which context, across matches. The number a sponsor asks for at renewal, produced automatically.

Hardware

We work with the equipment you already own

Team heart-rate systems and GPS vests from the major suppliers, read directly. We do not ask a club to replace what it already bought.

Delivery

On the club's own servers if needed

Athlete data is sensitive. The platform can run inside the club, with role-based access for medical, coaching and management.
